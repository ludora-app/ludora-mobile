# N. Decision title, as a short sentence

- **Status**: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [ADR-XXXX](XXXX-title.md)
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Source**: where the decision was taken (ticket, PR, discussion)

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, what exists today. Facts, not opinions.

## Decision

What we do, in the present tense ("The app reads…", "Features import…"). Precise enough to be checked
against the code.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what the backend or other people must do. Include the
downsides.

## Alternatives considered

The options set aside, and why.

## Open questions

Optional: what is still to be decided, and by whom. Remove the section when empty.
