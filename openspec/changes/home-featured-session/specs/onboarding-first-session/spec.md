## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Home reminder card

**Reason**: Decision A6 of the home redesign (SCRUM-712): until the player has joined or created a session, the
home screen features one session (the matching session, otherwise the prepared session) instead of a reminder
card. Without any sport, the home screen proposes nothing but "Créer un match".

**Migration**: See the `home` capability, requirements "Featured card priority", "Featured matching session",
"Featured prepared session" and "Empty profile". The "Sessions pour toi" and prepared session screens stay
reachable from the on-boarding.
